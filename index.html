Akihabara for Victor

Wed 25 Nov10:00 – 13:00Meet: Vie de France, BiTO AKIBA PLAZA 1F

Made for Victor and Romina · two guests from Monterrey, Mexico · Guide: Yuuki · Length: 3 hours

The plan

Wednesday 25 November, 10:00 to 13:00. I meet the two of you at Vie de France, on the ground floor of BiTO AKIBA PLAZA, 1-18-18 Sotokanda — the building right in front of the Electric Town exit of Akihabara station. We finish back at Akihabara station. The four shops, the map and the lunch ideas are all below — everything is on foot, inside one square kilometre.

What the day is for

EvangelionFrierenJoJoBocchi the Rock!Pokémon

Anime goods and figures, second-hand hunting, a game centre — and food. Each stop is there for a reason: it matches what you told us you love.

On the day

The plan is set; the pace is ours. You do not have to buy anything — looking is half the fun. Want only Pokémon cards? Swap in Hareruya 2 (Pokémon cards, 3 min away). Want manga? Book Off is one minute from the station.

Where we eat — we pick together

You told us food is half the fun — so lunch is not booked and not decided. That is the plan. Six places near the station, all open at lunchtime on a Wednesday: grilled meat, chicken in iron pots, steak, set meals, a diner and omurice. We look, we talk, we choose — by appetite. And if you would rather keep shopping and hunting, we skip lunch and stay longer at the shops. Tap a tile for details.

Hours from the Akihabara eatmap and Tabelog, checked on 10 Nov 2026. Lunch photos are examples, not always the shops themselves.

Good for

  • Anime goods, figures and second-hand treasures, all within a few minutes’ walk
  • Street food and sweets hunters — lunch is six places near the station, picked together on the day
  • Fans of Evangelion, Frieren, JoJo and Pokémon

Keep in mind
