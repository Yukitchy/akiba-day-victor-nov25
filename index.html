秋葉原アニメ
Wed 25 Nov · 10:00 to 13:00 Meet at Vie de France, BiTO AKIBA 1F

Made for Victor · for you and a friend · Guide: Yuuki · Length: 3 hours

01The plan

Wednesday 25 November. I meet the two of you at Vie de France, 10:00, on the ground floor of BiTO AKIBA — the building right in front of the Electric Town exit of Akihabara station. Then four shops picked for your favourites, all on foot inside one square kilometre, lunch together, and we finish back at Akihabara station, 13:00.

02What the day is for

ONE PIECE · GarpFrieren · StarkBlack Clover · YamiPokémon cards

Figures, anime goods, capsule toys and Pokémon cards. Each shop is there for a reason: it is where your favourites are easiest to find.

03On the day

The plan is set; the pace is ours. You do not have to buy anything — looking is half the fun. Want only Pokémon cards? Swap in Hareruya 2 (Pokémon cards, 3 min away). Want manga? Book Off is one minute from the station.

THE DAY

10:00 – 13:00

One plan, in the order we will live it. Times can breathe a little on the day.

  • 10:00Meet at Vie de France, BiTO AKIBA 1FI wait inside this bakery cafe on the ground floor, right in front of the Electric Town exit of Akihabara station.

Where we eat — we pick together

Lunch is not booked and not decided — that is the plan. Six places near the station, all open at lunchtime on a Wednesday: grilled meat, chicken in iron pots, steak, set meals, a diner and omurice. We look, we talk, we choose — by appetite. If you would rather keep shopping, we skip lunch and stay longer at the shops. Tap a tile for details.

Hours from the Akihabara eatmap and Tabelog, checked on 10 Nov 2026. Lunch photos are examples, not always the shops themselves.
